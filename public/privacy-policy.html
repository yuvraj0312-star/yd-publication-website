YD Publication ("we", "us", "our") operates the website ydapp.in and the YD App (package: in.ydapp.app). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, mobile number, and/or email address used for login (OTP-based).
  • Payment information: processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details on our servers.
  • Usage data: which semester/subject content you access, app usage patterns, and device information, used to improve our services.
  • Contact form submissions: name, email, and message when you reach out to us.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide access to purchased study material.
  • To process payments and send purchase confirmations.
  • To respond to your queries and provide customer support.
  • To send you relevant updates about new semesters, subjects, or offers (you can opt out anytime).
  • To improve our app and website based on usage patterns.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share data with:

  • Payment processors (Razorpay) to complete transactions.
  • Cloud service providers (AWS) for secure hosting and storage.
  • Law enforcement or government authorities, only if legally required.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted storage and secure servers (AWS Mumbai region), to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting us at support@ydapp.in.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for college-level students. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of our services after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us
